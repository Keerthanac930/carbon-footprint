Climate change represents one of the most pressing global challenges of our time, with individual carbon footprints varying significantly based on lifestyle choices, energy consumption patterns, transportation habits, and consumption behaviors. This project presents a comprehensive AI-driven Carbon Footprint Calculator & Prediction System that addresses the critical need for accessible, accurate, and actionable tools to help individuals understand, monitor, and reduce their environmental impact. The system is architected using a modern, scalable technology stack that includes React.js 18.2.0 for the frontend user interface, FastAPI 0.104.1 for high-performance backend API development, MySQL 8.0+ for robust relational database management, and a sophisticated Random Forest machine learning model trained on extensive synthetic residential carbon data with over 100 features covering household characteristics, energy consumption patterns, transportation behaviors, lifestyle choices, and environmental factors. The machine learning pipeline incorporates advanced feature engineering techniques including polynomial transformations, ratio calculations, interaction features, and categorical encoding, resulting in over 50 engineered features that capture complex relationships between user behaviors and carbon emissions. The Random Forest regressor model achieves exceptional predictive accuracy with 99.95% precision (R² = 0.9995), demonstrating superior performance with a mean absolute error of less than 87 kg CO₂ per year and a root mean square error of approximately 1,300 kg CO₂ per year, validated through rigorous 5-fold cross-validation that consistently yields R² scores above 0.9994. The platform delivers a comprehensive suite of features including real-time carbon footprint calculation with instant results, personalized and actionable recommendations for emission reduction tailored to individual user profiles, an intuitive six-section step-by-step interface that guides users through household information, energy usage, transportation patterns, climate and lifestyle factors, waste and consumption habits, and personal demographics, historical tracking and analytics capabilities that enable users to monitor their carbon footprint trends over time, interactive data visualization through charts and graphs powered by Chart.js and Recharts, secure user authentication and session management with bcrypt password hashing and JWT token-based security, responsive design ensuring seamless functionality across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices, and a Flutter-based mobile application for iOS and Android platforms. The system architecture follows a three-tier design pattern with clear separation of concerns, implementing service layer patterns, repository abstractions, and RESTful API endpoints that support concurrent user access with response times under 200 milliseconds, capable of handling over 100 simultaneous users while maintaining system stability and performance. The application integrates advanced machine learning preprocessing pipelines that automatically transform user inputs into the feature space required by the trained model, ensuring consistent and accurate predictions regardless of input variations. Beyond core calculation functionality, the platform includes gamification elements with eco-badges and point systems to encourage sustainable behaviors, a digital twin avatar that adapts to user environmental choices, OCR bill scanning capabilities for automatic product emission calculation, an AI chatbot for interactive eco-tips and explanations, global statistics comparing regional carbon footprints, environmental widgets displaying live air quality and weather data, goal tracking features for emission reduction targets, community leaderboards for social engagement, and a carbon marketplace for offset opportunities. The project demonstrates practical application of full-stack web development principles, machine learning engineering, database design and optimization, user experience design, and environmental science integration, serving as a comprehensive solution that bridges the gap between complex environmental data and user-friendly actionable insights. By providing individuals with accurate, personalized, and engaging tools to understand their carbon footprint, the system empowers users to make informed decisions about their environmental impact, adopt sustainable lifestyle changes, and contribute meaningfully to global climate change mitigation efforts, ultimately fostering greater environmental awareness and promoting collective action toward a more sustainable future.
